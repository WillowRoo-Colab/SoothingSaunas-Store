# Document Authority

This document defines the implementation philosophy of Soothing Saunas.

AI systems and human developers shall implement these principles as written.

If implementation conflicts arise, report the conflict rather than redefining these principles.

---

# Purpose

The objective of the Soothing Saunas platform is not simply to sell products.

The platform exists to help guests make confident wellness decisions through education, transparency, and respectful guidance.

---

# Owner's Founding Motivation

The owner's professional background is in nursing — a career chosen because of a genuine desire to see people live happier, healthier, more complete lives: pain-free, stress-free, clear-headed, centered, and focused. Soothing Saunas exists as an extension of that motivation, not a departure from it.

This has direct implementation consequences:

- The guest's health and wellbeing is a genuine priority, held equal to or above revenue. Implementation choices shall reflect that ordering honestly, not as a marketing claim.
- The brand should read as high-end, rich, and durable — products and presentation that feel like a considered, worthwhile investment rather than an impulse purchase.
- The team behind the platform should come across as trustworthy and genuinely invested in outcomes, not as a storefront optimized purely for conversion.
- The site should function as a real educational resource — somewhere a guest can learn something new or refresh existing knowledge — not merely as a catalog with supporting copy.

---

# Core Principles

- Educate before selling.
- Confidence is the primary outcome.
- Products are vehicles, not the mission.
- Trust takes precedence over conversion.
- Every interaction should leave the guest better informed.
- Long-term relationships are more valuable than individual transactions.
- Clarity always outweighs marketing language.

---

# Guest Experience Requirements

- Every page should reduce uncertainty.
- Explain why a recommendation is appropriate.
- Explain who a product is and is not for.
- Support decisions with evidence when appropriate.
- Respect the guest's autonomy.

---

# Prohibited Behaviors

The following practices are prohibited:

- Manipulative urgency or countdown timers.
- Misleading scarcity.
- Hidden fees or misleading pricing.
- Aggressive pop-ups or forced interruptions.
- Dark UX patterns designed to pressure purchases.

---

# Decision Hierarchy

When competing solutions exist, prioritize them in the following order:

1. Guest trust
2. Education
3. Clarity
4. Accessibility
5. Long-term relationship
6. Conversion

---

# Implementation Directives

## MUST

- Provide educational context before major purchasing decisions whenever practical.
- Present accurate and transparent information.
- Use plain language where possible.
- Respect guest choice.

---

## SHOULD

- Link related educational resources.
- Encourage informed comparison.
- Design for confidence rather than urgency.

---

## MUST NOT

- Increase conversion by reducing transparency.
- Recommend products inconsistent with guest needs.
- Prioritize sales metrics over guest trust.
