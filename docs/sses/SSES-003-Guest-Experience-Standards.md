# Purpose

This specification establishes the minimum guest experience standards for the Soothing Saunas platform.

Every customer-facing interface shall prioritize education, trust, clarity, and confidence.

---

# Owner Directive

If forced to choose between higher conversions and better guest understanding, always prioritize guest understanding.
