# Purpose

This specification establishes the minimum guest experience standards for the Soothing Saunas platform.

Every customer-facing interface shall prioritize education, trust, clarity, and confidence.

---

# First Impression Standard

The homepage is a guest's first exposure to the brand and shall be treated as the most deliberately crafted surface on the site.

- The homepage shall feel welcoming, calm, and memorable — the beginning of a guest's Soothing Saunas experience, not a generic storefront landing page.
- The homepage shall favor the dark-dominant treatment described in SSES-007 so it reads as immersive and relaxing rather than bright or promotional.
- The homepage shall not be visually overstimulating under low-light or relaxed viewing conditions (for example, a guest browsing late at night or while unwinding). Avoid harsh contrast, jarring brightness, or aggressive motion.
- A guest should come away from the homepage feeling that what follows is a considered, worthwhile investment in their wellbeing — not a transaction being pushed on them.

---

# Owner Directive

If forced to choose between higher conversions and better guest understanding, always prioritize guest understanding.
